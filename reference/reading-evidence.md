# Reading the research evidence

These are the author’s research accounts, edited into articles with links to preserved notebooks and public artifacts. “Reviewed” in earlier descriptions refers to interpretation and publication review in this research workflow. It does **not** establish external peer review or independent reproduction. Automated source, link, export, and publication checks verify the publication machinery, not the scientific conclusion.

## Dates and revisions

The article date is its first public article date. **Research period** describes when the investigation occurred. A later publication-context note records substantive documentation changes. The September 29 date shared by the 13 milestone articles is a publication date, not 13 experiments performed on one day. Source history retains previous article versions; source notebooks remain byte-for-byte preserved.

## What each kind of evidence establishes

- A formal checkpoint establishes its mathematical statement under its stated assumptions. A Lean source link permits inspection; an empirical interpretation still needs measurement.
- A historical observation reports a result under the described comparison. Missing counts, splits, seeds, or uncertainty procedures remain missing; do not infer them from a toy example or another experiment.
- A successful execution says the run completed. Package verification establishes integrity and declared structure. Neither alone establishes the scientific hypothesis or independent reproduction.
- A teaching example is a small illustration. Saved example output and output from your browser session are separate from historical measurements.

## Why the shared browser model is present

The small trained baseline is a common instrument for inspecting the representation and readout across articles. Its prominence is not a claim that it is the best architecture. It is not each article’s intervention, benchmark, or exact reproduction. In particular, the checkpoint-controlled result in milestone 006 favored shared recurrence over the unit-hypersphere variant. The article’s own evidence and limitations govern its claim.
