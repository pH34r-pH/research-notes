# Research Notes

Research Notes is the public narrative layer of an ongoing research program on how neural systems **represent, preserve, transform, expose, and use information**.

The notebooks are ordered by the development of the questions rather than by topic. Positive results, negative results, abandoned experiments, and later corrections are preserved because the path by which an interpretation failed is part of the research record.

## The recurring distinction

A central thread is the separation between:

1. information present in a learned state;
2. information recoverable from that state;
3. information the model's native consumer actually uses;
4. information that causally affects behavior.

Those categories can diverge.
