# How to read the project

The public notebooks are research milestones, not a linear course and not a claim that every early interpretation survived.

## Recommended path

Read the numbered notebooks in order when you want the research story. Use the concept/reference pages when a term or method becomes important. Use the Visual Intuition Atlas for synthetic geometric intuition.

Each milestone is organized around:

```text
question
  -> experiment or derivation
  -> observation/result
  -> interpretation boundary
  -> later control/literature/correction
  -> narrower next question
```

## Evidence markers

- **✓ Formal checkpoint** — a mathematical result under explicit assumptions.
- **● Observation** — an empirical result under a specified protocol.
- **◇ Assumption** — a premise used but not established in that step.
- **? Hypothesis** — an explanation or prediction still open to testing.

The markers prevent one kind of evidence from quietly borrowing the strength of another.

## “What this does not show”

Every mature milestone makes its strongest non-claim explicit. This is especially important for striking endpoints: a large effect can still fail to identify mechanism, generalize, or survive a better control.

## Reconstruction dates

The public notebooks may be reconstructed after the underlying research period. Research date and public reconstruction date remain separate; Git history is not rewritten to simulate earlier publication.
