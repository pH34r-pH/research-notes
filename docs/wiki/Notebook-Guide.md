# Notebook guide

The numbered notebooks are the primary narrative.

## 001–004 — representations and matched operations

These introduce text-as-signal experiments, locate where representational loss occurs, distinguish coordinate systems from operations, and isolate a phase-aware mechanism.

## 005–008 — normalization and hypersphere geometry

These present the surprising endpoint, document why it can mislead, derive normalization behavior before further training, and use frozen interventions/derivatives to bound the mechanism.

## 009 — theorem-ledger method

Explains why proof-first decomposition became part of the research workflow: if a mathematical claim can be settled exactly, do that before spending an experiment on it.

## 010–011 — sufficient state and geometry from invariance

Develop the dynamic quotient / predictive-state question and examine which geometries follow from the invariances a reasoner should preserve.

## 012–013 — natural-source distinctions and utilization

Move toward task-aligned natural-source evidence and the distinction between information that can be read from a representation and information the native consumer actually exploits.

## Visual Intuition Atlas

The Atlas contains small synthetic examples and plots for difficult geometry/representation ideas. It is explanatory, not a substitute for the experimental evidence.
