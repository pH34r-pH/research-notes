# Research Notes

This project preserves the chronological research notebooks and publishes reviewed, mature explanations as canonical MyST articles. The article pages carry the narrative, evidence boundaries, figures, citations, and clearly marked synthetic teaching cells; the notebooks remain available as historical and computational source records.

Start with [Milestone 001 — What if text were a signal?](articles/001-text-as-signal.md), or go to the [Research Notes publication map](PUBLICATION-DISPOSITIONS.md) for every notebook, article, and reference disposition.

The numbered article sequence follows the research chronology and links back to source notebooks. Standalone articles cover infrastructure and open questions without implying a new completed milestone. Later work retains uncertainty and negative results from the earlier record.
